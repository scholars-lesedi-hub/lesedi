Help for parents and teachers

Read prompts and options aloud if your child needs help. Invite the child to name or point to the picture before choosing. Explain new words with a familiar object or gesture. A familiar language can help explain the meaning.

Explore 96 word activities across sixteen groups, including number words, feelings, actions and opposites. Build 48 sentences, then answer 48 questions across sixteen short original stories. Some word activities use a short spoken clue as well as a picture: read the clue aloud without saying the answer first. For feelings, discuss that people can feel differently; answers here match the stated story clues. Position sentences use imagination prompts rather than diagrams. Colours require a colour screen. Work for 5–10 minutes at a time; take a break whenever your child needs one. Use the activity selector to revisit a favourite task.

Stars show tasks completed correctly, including after a retry. They are encouragement, not a formal reading grade. Oral language, pronunciation and handwriting are not assessed. No recorded audio is included; an adult can read aloud.

This is an independent Lesedi starter, not a complete reading course or a verified mapping to any country’s syllabus. Pictures are symbols; use real objects where they help. Progress stays only while this page is open and is not saved to an account.
