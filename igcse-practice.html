Waiting for the bus

Amantle packed her bag the night before. She arrived while the bus station was still quiet. The timetable had faded in the sun, and she did not want to miss the only morning bus. When the engine finally started, its roar swallowed the chatter on the platform.

Optional writing practice and self-check

In 80–120 words, continue the scene from Amantle’s point of view. Use a sound or sight to show how she feels as the bus leaves.

Check that you kept the point of view consistent, used a specific sensory detail, and showed her feelings through what she notices or does.
