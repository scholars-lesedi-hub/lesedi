What works offline?

Downloaded activity pictures, reading, supported practice checks, guidance and prepared documents work here. It cannot create new AI material, process OCR, renew a package, send a message, update a forum or upload exam results.

Offline practice is not an exam submission. For formal offline exams, use the separate downloaded examination player and export completed answers to your teacher. There is no automatic results synchronisation from this learning library.

Downloaded learning copies can be retained after subscription expiry; new downloads require the website’s applicable access check. Use resources privately; do not resell or redistribute them publicly.
